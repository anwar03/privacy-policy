Privacy Policy — Online Shopping Assistant For AliExpress

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Online Shopping Assistant for AliExpress ("the extension") is designed to help you evaluate products while shopping on AliExpress. We take your privacy seriously. In short: the extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and it has no analytics or tracking.

What the extension does

When you open an AliExpress product or listing page, the extension:

  1. Reads the page's publicly visible product information (such as rating, review count, review sentiment, and store feedback) directly in your browser.
  2. Requests public product-review statistics from AliExpress's own endpoint (feedback.aliexpress.com) to calculate a 0–10 quality score.
  3. Displays that score, a seller-trust indicator, and a "safe to buy" summary on the page.

All of this happens locally in your browser. The product ID used to look up review statistics is sent only to AliExpress itself — the same website you are already visiting — and never to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit any personal or personally identifiable information. We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party service. We do not access your browsing history, location, contacts, credentials, or activity on other websites. The extension only runs on AliExpress pages.

Local storage

The extension may use your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) to remember your preferences and to cache product-analysis results on your own device so pages load faster. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension.
