AliScore Online Shopping Assistant For AliExpress

Last updated: 23 August 2026

AliScore Online Shopping Assistant For AliExpress ("the extension") helps you evaluate products while shopping on AliExpress. In short: the extension does not collect an account, a profile, or any personally identifying information about you, and it contains no analytics or tracking. The sections below describe exactly what it reads and what leaves your browser.

No personal data collected No tracking or analytics Runs only on AliExpress

What the extension does

When you open an AliExpress product, listing, cart or checkout page, the extension:

  1. Reads the page's publicly visible product information (such as rating, review count, review sentiment, and store feedback) directly in your browser.
  2. Requests public product-review statistics for the product you are viewing, so it can calculate a 0–10 quality score. See Requests through our servers below for how that request travels.
  3. Displays that score, a seller-trust indicator, and a "safe to buy" summary on the page.
  4. May add our affiliate code to AliExpress product links and — only if you have explicitly opted in — may re-open an AliExpress product page through that link, which reloads the page once. See Affiliate links below.

The extension runs only on AliExpress domains (aliexpress.com and its regional variants, aliexpress.ru, and aliexpress.us). It never runs on, reads, or accesses any other website.

Requests through our servers

To look up review statistics, the extension sends the product identifier of the page you are viewing to AliExpress's public review endpoint (feedback.aliexpress.com). That request is sent either directly from your browser, or forwarded through relay servers we operate at crwler-*.ecomstal.com. The relay exists for one technical reason: it spreads the lookups across several network paths so AliExpress's anti-bot protection does not rate-limit or challenge your shopping session.

What the relay receives is limited to the product identifier being looked up. It receives no account information, no login state, no cookies from your AliExpress session, no browsing history, and nothing that identifies you. As with any request to any web server, our server sees the network (IP) address the request arrives from; we do not use it to identify you, do not link it to any identity or profile, and do not sell or share it.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit any personal or personally identifiable information. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking pixel, and no third-party analytics or advertising service. We do not access your browsing history, location, contacts, credentials, or your activity on any non-AliExpress website.

Cookies

The extension does not create, set, or modify any cookie. It does read two of AliExpress's own non-private cookies (aeu_cid and xman_us_f) on AliExpress pages, for a single purpose: these cookies record which affiliate referral AliExpress currently has on file, and reading them is how the extension can tell you that another installed extension has replaced that referral (see Affiliate links). This read happens locally in your browser and the cookie values are never transmitted anywhere. AliExpress's login and session cookies are marked httpOnly, which means the extension is technically incapable of reading them at all.

Local storage

The extension may use your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) to remember your preferences from the extension popup, including your answer to the affiliate-link dialog described below. It also uses your browser's per-tab sessionStorage to count how many times it has re-opened a given product page, so it can stop after three. All of this stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension.

Affiliate links

Some AliExpress product links shown or enhanced by the extension may include our affiliate tracking code. If you make a purchase through such a link, we may earn a small commission from AliExpress at no extra cost to you — prices never change.

Other installed extensions can replace that code with their own. The extension handles this in two ways, and the difference matters:

1. Only after you opt in. The first time you open an AliExpress product page, the extension asks — in a dialog on the page, with two buttons — whether you will allow it to open product pages through our affiliate link. Nothing happens unless you press “Allow”. If you press “No thanks”, or simply ignore the dialog, the extension never re-directs or reloads any page.

2. What happens if you do opt in. On an AliExpress product page where our affiliate code is not the one currently applied, the extension will, about one second after the page loads, re-open that same product page through our affiliate link. This reloads the product page once. You stay on the same product; nothing about the product, the price, or your cart changes. It will not do this more than three times for the same product, and it does not do it at all on your cart, checkout, or order pages — those are never redirected.

You can switch this off at any time from the extension popup (“Support us with affiliate links”), and switching it off stops it immediately.

We are describing this plainly because it is a visible behaviour: if a product page reloads itself shortly after opening, that is this feature, and it is the only thing in the extension that does it.

All affiliate tracking is handled entirely by AliExpress's own affiliate system: no personal information about you is sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see who you are, what you bought, or any of your account details.
