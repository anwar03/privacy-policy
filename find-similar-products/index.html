Find Similar Products - Search by Image

Last updated: 24 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)

Find Similar Products - Search by Image is a browser extension that finds products on AliExpress, Alibaba.com and 1688.com that look like an image you choose, and similar listings on Amazon and eBay. This policy says what the extension reads, where it keeps it, and what leaves your computer.

Short version: your images go from your browser straight to Alibaba Group's own search services, never to us. Amazon and eBay never receive your image, only the title of the best match, as an ordinary store search. Everything the extension saves — recent searches, saved products, price history, settings — stays in your browser on your computer. There is no account and no advertising. Two things are not local, and this policy states both: AliExpress links you click carry our affiliate tag, and anonymous usage counts are sent to our server only after you agree. Neither carries your images, searches, name, address or browsing history.

What the extension handles, and where it goes

1. Images you choose to search

A search starts only when you act: the search button on an image you point at, the right-click item on an image, selecting an area of the page, pasting or dropping an image, picking a file, or giving the address of an image.

  • The image is converted inside your browser and sent to Alibaba Group's search services to find matching products: open-s.alibaba.com, www.alibaba.com, AliExpress's own search (acs.aliexpress.com and AliExpress pages) and 1688's (s.1688.com, h5api.m.1688.com).
  • A site you untick in the popup or the settings page is never sent your image, neither in a search you run nor in a price check.
  • Only your default site (AliExpress unless you pick another in the popup) is searched straight away. The other image sites are sent your image only when you open their tab on the results page.
  • It is held in the browser's session storage only so the results page can show and retry that search. Session storage is cleared when you close the browser.
  • It is never sent to us.

Amazon and eBay: a product title, not your image

Amazon and eBay have no image search the extension can use, so they are searched by text, and only when you open their tab on the results page:

  • The title of the best AliExpress (or, failing that, Alibaba.com) match is cut to its first few words and searched on Amazon and eBay, as if you had typed it into their search box. Your image is never sent to Amazon or eBay.
  • The store is the one for the country you pick in Settings ("Amazon & eBay country"; the first guess comes from your browser's language), for example amazon.de and ebay.de.
  • The request is made from your browser with your own cookies for that store, as an ordinary visit to its search page would be. If you are signed in there, the store can link the search to your account, as it would for a search you typed.
  • The result page is read inside the extension to list the products; nothing from it is sent to us.
  • Untick Amazon or eBay in the popup or Settings and it is not contacted.

3. Price tracking — off unless you switch it on

With tracking on, every few hours the extension re-runs the ordinary image search for at most four saved products, using each product's own photo. A saved Amazon or eBay product is checked instead by searching its own title on the store it came from. It contacts nothing beyond the search services in section 1 and the site that hosts the photo. If a price falls far enough it can show a browser notification; that permission is optional and asked for separately.

4. The button on images

When you switch it on and grant access to all sites (below), a small script is added to pages you visit so the button is there when your pointer reaches a picture. It watches where your pointer is and draws a button. It reads no page content and sends nothing until you press it. Switch either off and the script is removed.

5. Cookies and request headers

  • Alibaba's search API accepts a request only with Alibaba's own cna cookie. The extension reads it on alibaba.com and, if it is missing, asks Alibaba to issue one.
  • AliExpress's and 1688's search services require a token from their own _m_h5_tk cookie on their API hosts to sign a request. The extension reads it there and sends it only back to that same service.
  • For 1688, the extension sets the Referer and Origin headers that 1688's service expects. This applies only to the extension's own search requests to 1688, never to pages you browse.
  • Searches on Amazon and eBay (section 1) carry your own cookies for that store, as a visit to its search page would. The extension does not read those cookies.

None of these values is stored by the extension or sent to us.

6. Affiliate links on AliExpress

This is how the extension is paid for. It is on by default in the Chrome version, and you can switch it off in Settings → Affiliate links.

  • On AliExpress pages (aliexpress.com, aliexpress.us), when you press on a link to an AliExpress product page that no one has tagged, the extension changes it at that moment to go to the same product through our AliExpress affiliate link (s.click.aliexpress.com).
  • Product links in the extension's own results, side panel, comparison and saved pages go through our affiliate link the same way.
  • A link that already carries another affiliate partner's code, or an influencer or creator link, is never changed. AliExpress home, welcome and new-user pages are never sent through our link.
  • The extension never opens, redirects or reloads a page on its own; a link only takes effect when you click it.
  • Switched off, no link is changed, including on AliExpress pages already open; links the extension had already changed there are put back.
  • The product, the seller and the price you pay are unchanged. If AliExpress pays a commission on your purchase, it comes out of AliExpress's margin, not your price.
  • The affiliate link is part of the extension's package. Building it sends no request to us. When the usage counts below are on, the extension notes locally that a link was changed — not which link — and that note, and whether affiliate links are switched on, are part of the counts.
  • The Firefox version contains no affiliate code at all.

7. Anonymous usage counts — only if you agree

The welcome page asks once. Only if you choose Allow does the extension send, to our own server at shoppingassistant.xyz and at most every half hour:

  • that this install is still active, once a day;
  • when you open an AliExpress product page: the product number, and whether the visit came through our affiliate link, another partner's (with that partner's link code), or none;
  • when you open an AliExpress checkout page and when an order is confirmed: whose affiliate link, if any, the visit carried. An order is identified only by a one-way hash of its number mixed with your random install id; we cannot turn it back into an AliExpress order number.

Each batch also carries the extension's version, the browser's name and a random install id created on your device. No images, searches, saved products, prices, names, addresses, page addresses or browsing history are sent, and there is no account. You can switch the counts off at any time in Settings → Usage counts. That throws away anything not yet sent and asks our server to delete what this install already sent. The Firefox version never sends these counts.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than the extension's features, and not for advertising or to determine creditworthiness.
  • We do not read, record or send your browsing history.
  • We keep nothing on our server except the usage counts you allowed, and those are deleted when you switch them off.

Third parties

Alibaba Group (Alibaba.com, AliExpress, 1688.com) receives the images you search and the search requests, because that is where the products are. Amazon and eBay receive the title searches in section 1, with your cookies for their site. Their handling of it is governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The extension is a shopping tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If what the extension handles changes, this page is updated before the new version is published, and the extension asks again before sending anything new.
